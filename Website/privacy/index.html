Privacy Policy

How DevSpireHub handles information, respects user privacy, and manages data transparency across our website.

Last Updated: March 2026 • Published by DevSpireHub

Operational Transparency Notice: This policy is a plain-language explanation of how DevSpireHub functions technically and handles data today. DevSpireHub does not operate user accounts, databases of personal passwords, or paywalled profile systems. Our goal is to provide useful engineering knowledge while maintaining minimal data retention.

1. Introduction

Welcome to DevSpireHub (https://devspirehub.com), an independent technical learning platform founded and maintained by Kumaradoss S, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

DevSpireHub is designed as a free, open-access technical platform. We do not require accounts, logins, passwords, or paid subscriptions to access our programming tutorials, systems guides, or cheat sheets. This policy outlines what information is handled when you browse the site, submit a contact inquiry, or use our developer utilities.

2. Information Collected

We process information in three distinct functional contexts:

2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Inquiries: When you submit a question or feedback via our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, message subject, and the message body. This information is processed by Web3Forms and delivered directly to our operational email inbox.
  • Newsletter Subscription: If you voluntarily subscribe to the optional DevSpire Dispatch newsletter, your email address is submitted directly to our email provider (Brevo) to deliver tutorial updates.

2.2 Information Processed Automatically by Infrastructure

When your browser requests a webpage on DevSpireHub, standard web server and CDN request logs are generated automatically to facilitate transmission, maintain security, and prevent denial-of-service attacks:

  • IP address (used transiently for routing and edge security filtering)
  • Browser type, engine version, and operating system
  • Referral URL (the page that linked you to DevSpireHub)
  • Requested resource path and HTTP status code
  • Timestamp of request

2.3 Third-Party Services Data

We integrate specific third-party platforms for edge distribution, aggregated analytics, and monetization:

  • Cloudflare: Provides CDN caching, TLS encryption, and edge firewall filtering.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) / Google Tag Manager: Collects anonymized, aggregated traffic metrics (page views, session duration, general geography) to help us understand which tutorials are useful.
  • Google AdSense: Serves display advertisements to offset hosting and domain costs. AdSense uses cookies to serve contextual or personalized ads.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed strictly for functional, operational, and communication purposes:

Data Context Specific Purpose Operational Justification
Contact Messages Replying to reader questions, bug reports, and collaboration requests Direct Communication
Newsletter Subscriptions Sending tutorial releases and platform announcements via Brevo Consent-Based Delivery
Server & CDN Request Logs DDoS mitigation, routing, resource caching, and service uptime Infrastructure Security
Aggregated Analytics Evaluating article readership trends and improving site performance Content Optimization
Display Advertising Offsetting server, CDN, and domain operating expenses Platform Sustainability

4. Cookies & Tracking

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser by a website. DevSpireHub does not use first-party cookies for session authentication, as we do not provide user accounts.

Client-Side Storage: Interactive tools such as NetPulse use browser localStorage purely on your local device to retain recent speed test results between page reloads. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to DevSpireHub or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

5. Third-Party Services

DevSpireHub partners with trusted external service providers to ensure fast, secure, and reliable platform delivery:

  • Cloudflare: Acts as a reverse proxy, CDN, and security perimeter. Cloudflare processes request metadata and operates under strict privacy and security standards.
  • Google Analytics 4: Measures high-level reader traffic without storing raw IP addresses permanently.
  • Google AdSense: Provides contextual display advertisements.
  • Web3Forms: Serverless gateway that routes contact form submissions to our email inbox without storing customer databases.
  • Brevo: Compliant email marketing provider hosting our optional newsletter distribution list.

6. External Links

DevSpireHub includes outbound links to official software documentation, GitHub repositories, RFC standards, and third-party developer resources. We have no control over the privacy practices, cookie policies, or content of external websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit from our links.

7. Data Security

We employ standard industry measures to protect the integrity and security of our website:

  • Zero User Account Database: Because DevSpireHub does not store user logins, passwords, or payment cards, the risk of credential exposure on our infrastructure is inherently minimized.
  • Full HTTPS/TLS Encryption: All traffic between your browser and DevSpireHub is encrypted in transit using modern TLS protocols.
  • Edge Security Filtering: DDoS mitigation and web application firewalls protect our origin servers from unauthorized intrusion.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as functionally necessary:

  • Server & CDN Logs: Ephemeral server logs are routinely rotated and purged by infrastructure providers.
  • Contact Inquiries: Correspondence sent to our operational inbox is retained only for as long as needed to address your feedback, question, or errata report.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Maintained in Brevo until you click the unsubscribe link in any email footer or request removal.

9. User Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, DevSpireHub respects your control over your personal information:

  • Right to Inquire & Delete: You may request the deletion of any prior email correspondence or inquiry by contacting us directly.
  • Right to Unsubscribe: You can cancel your subscription to DevSpire Dispatch at any time using the one-click unsubscribe link provided in every newsletter.
  • Cookie Control: You can block or clear cookies at any time via your browser settings without losing access to our educational content.

10. Policy Updates

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect updates to our technical architecture, third-party integrations, or applicable industry practices. When changes occur, the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document will be updated. Continued use of DevSpireHub following revisions constitutes awareness of the updated practices.
